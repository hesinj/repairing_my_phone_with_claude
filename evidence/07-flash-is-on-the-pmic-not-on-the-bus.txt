## The phone's flash is driven by the PMIC (SPMI), not by I2C bus 3. Device-tree excerpts (decompiled with dtc) and the matching kernel log lines.
## Device tree: prebuilt/dtbo.img (sha256 2a89bcd7b432bec6b07afd5b093baad5b0e05e325a3c42158c241dcc9b4bdd47) and prebuilt/dtb
## (sha256 831d9ffe0b831d44279cb5236998afe7fde83ace728cbb50f5c16319a6fa9b4e) of https://github.com/hesinj/orangefox-toco; the README of that
## repository says they are byte-identical to the ones in PixelOS's images. dtbo.img holds one overlay (starting at byte 64), dtb one base tree.
## How: cut each file at the FDT magic d00dfeed (python: data.find(b'\xd0\x0d\xfe\xed')), then `dtc -I dtb -O dts`. In the overlay a reference to a node
## of the base tree is written 0xffffffff; the `__fixups__` lines of Part B say which base node each reference gets. Tabs are kept; nothing is edited.
## Kernel log: the saved console pstore-before-boot-32.txt of the black-box recorder (release kernel 4.14.356). CAM_ERR is only the log level of that
## message; its text says `rc 0`.
##
## What this shows: the rear flash and torch nodes take their LEDs from the PM6150L PMIC's flash module; the AW3644 and the camera's i2c_flash devices
## are on 890000.i2c, the bus that does not answer, and are only an extra `soft-led-flash-src` of the rear sensors. The camera sensors use the CCI.
## What it does not show: what the extra LED does for the camera, whether it works, or whether a camera app opens the rear cameras with the bus dead.

## Two kernel experiments on the camera, each started with `fastboot boot` (nothing was written to the phone). Same Android, same camera app.
##

## sensors have no soft flash. The kernel log shows no 'FXF led flash cell index' lines at boot and no flash init at open: the experiment ran.
## Result: it does not help. The camera software needs the soft flash for the main camera; without it the HAL aborts 0.5 s after the sensor
